# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) or credentials.
- Deliberately disruptive behaviour, including posting working exploits against downstream users in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at <info@axiomnode.tech>. Reports are handled confidentially.
